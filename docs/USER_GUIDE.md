## A. Introduction

### What JMS Sales App does

JMS Sales App is a mobile-first sales capture and business-performance platform built for small and growing businesses. It gives every business — a shop, a kiosk, a restaurant, a service counter — a simple daily workflow: **record every sale as it happens, keep a clean history of it, and see how the business is really performing.**

### Who it's for

Any business owner or manager who wants their team to log sales on a phone, in seconds, without spreadsheets or paper records — and who wants trustworthy reports at the end of the day, week, or month.

### Key strengths

- **Built for phones.** Every screen is designed mobile-first — fast to tap through on a phone at the counter, not a desktop tool squeezed onto a small screen.
- **One business, many people, one accurate picture.** Invite your team, control exactly what each person can see and do, and every sale they record rolls up into one shared, trustworthy view of the business.
- **Nothing recorded twice.** Sales are protected against accidental double-submission, and every correction, void, or reversal is tracked — never silently edited away.
- **Real reporting, not guesswork.** Daily summaries, product performance, and staff performance are generated automatically from what was actually sold.
- **Data stays private per business.** Every business's data is fully isolated from every other business on the platform.

### How it helps

From the moment a sale is made to the moment the owner checks performance, JMS Sales App connects the two: **capture → history → reports → decisions.**

## B. Getting Started

**The path:** Sign Up → Business Setup → Login → Dashboard.

### Step 1 — Sign Up

1. Open the app and tap **Sign up**.
2. Enter your first name, last name, business name, email, phone, country, and a password.
3. Tap **Create account**.

### Step 2 — Business Setup (Onboarding)

The first time you log in, you're guided through a short setup wizard:

1. **Business details** — business type, website (optional), currency, and timezone.
2. **Location & hours** — your primary location's name, address, and opening hours for each day of the week.

This only happens once. After it's complete, every future login goes straight to your dashboard.

### Step 3 — Login

Return any time at the **Login** page with your email and password.

If your account is assigned to more than one branch, you'll see a **Select a Branch** screen after logging in — choose which branch you're working from for this session. Everything you see and record (sales, business day, reports, analytics) is scoped to that branch until you log out. To work from a different branch, log out and log back in, then pick the other one — there's no in-app switcher. If you're only assigned to one branch, you're taken straight to your dashboard as usual.

### Step 4 — Dashboard

You land on **Capture Sales** — the main daily screen every user sees first. From here, a bottom navigation bar gives access to **Sales, History, Analytics, Reports,** and **More** (everything else).

## C. Setting Up the Business

Before your team starts recording sales, spend a few minutes setting the business up properly.

### Complete your business profile

Go to **More → Settings** to review business details, currency, and configuration options at any time — not just during onboarding.

### Configure business settings

Also under **More → Settings**, you can turn on/off:
- **Product ranking** (Gold/Silver/Bronze badges on top-selling products)
- **Daily sales volume** shown per product on the Capture Sales screen
- **Product price** visibility on the Capture Sales screen
- **Sale number format** — customize how sale reference numbers look
- **Business anniversary wishes** — an automatic or reviewed congratulatory message sent to your team on the business's anniversary

### Create and manage products

Go to **More → Products** — see section **F** below for the full walkthrough.

### Add and manage branches

If your business operates from more than one location, go to **More → Settings → Branches** to add additional branches beyond the one created during onboarding — give each a name and address. Existing branches can be edited or deactivated (never permanently deleted) from the same screen. Every sale, business day, and report is kept separate per branch — see section **D** below for assigning your team to specific branches, and **Step 3 — Login** for what a multi-branch team member sees when they sign in.

### Configure relevant sales settings

Under **More → Security**, you can additionally restrict login to business hours and/or your business's physical location (geofencing) — useful if you only want staff signing in while actually at work.

## D. Inviting Employees / Users

A Tenant Administrator can bring their team onto the platform in a few steps.

1. Go to **More → Users**.
2. Tap **Invite user**.
3. Enter their **full name**, **email**, and choose a **role** (see roles below).
4. If your business has more than one branch, choose whether this person works across **all branches** or only **specific branches** — this decides what they see after logging in (see **Step 3 — Login** above). Businesses with only one branch never see this step.
5. Tap **Invite**. The person receives an email invitation.
6. When they accept it, they set their own password and are added to your business — no further action needed from you.

A person's branch assignment can be changed later from the **Users** page at any time — the same "all branches" or "specific branches" choice applies.

### What invited users can access

Access is controlled entirely by **role**:
- **Sales User** — can record sales and view their own sales history/analytics. This is the role for staff who capture daily sales.
- **Supervisor** — everything a Sales User can do, plus broader visibility across the business (e.g. seeing everyone's sales, not just their own).
- **Tenant Administrator** — full access to every feature: products, users, roles, reports, settings, billing.

Roles and their exact permissions can be reviewed and customized under **More → Roles**.

### Confirming an invite worked

On the **Users** page, an invited person shows as **Invited** until they accept; once they log in for the first time, their status changes to **Active**. You can disable or re-enable any user at any time from the same screen.

Once active, **that employee can log in on their own device and immediately start recording their own daily sales** — this is the whole point of inviting them.

## E. Recording Sales

This is the screen your team will use the most.

1. Tap **Sales** in the bottom navigation. You'll see your product catalog as a scrollable list.
2. **Search** for a product by name using the search bar at the top if the list is long.
3. Tap a product to open **Record Sale**.
4. If the product isn't in your catalog, tap **Others** at the bottom of the list — a required **"Enter Product Name"** field appears so you can type exactly what was sold. The sale still records correctly and rolls up into reporting under that typed name.
5. Enter the **amount sold** (and **quantity**, if your business tracks it — this can be toggled on/off in Settings).
6. Tap **Record Sale**. You'll see an instant confirmation.
7. To review what's already been recorded, tap **History** in the bottom navigation at any time.

Every sale is protected from accidental duplicate submissions (e.g. a double-tap or a network retry never creates two records).

## F. Products

Go to **More → Products** to manage your catalog.

- **Creating a product** — tap the add-product field at the top, enter a name, price, and optional description, then save.
- **Editing a product** — tap a product's edit icon to change its name, price, or description.
- **Product images** — open a product to upload, replace, or remove its photo.
- **Activating/deactivating** — toggle a product's status without deleting it; inactive products are hidden from the Sales screen but keep their history.
- **Deleting** — a product can be permanently deleted only if it has never been sold; otherwise, deactivate it instead.
- **Searching** — use the search bar at the top of the Products page to instantly filter a long catalog.
- **Bulk upload** — for a large catalog, download the products template, fill in Product Name, Expected Price, and Image URL for each row, and upload it in one go.
- **On the Sales screen** — active products appear in the same order as this list, with an automatic **Others** entry always last, for anything not in your catalog.

## G. Users & Permissions

Go to **More → Users** to manage your team, and **More → Roles** to manage what each role can do.

- **Viewing users** — see everyone's name, email, status, and assigned role in one list.
- **Inviting new users** — see section **D** above.
- **User roles** — assign or change a user's role directly from the Users list.
- **Activating/deactivating** — disable a user's access instantly (e.g. when someone leaves) without deleting their sales history, or re-enable them later.
- **Editing a name** — a Tenant Administrator can correct a teammate's display name after they've accepted their invite.
- **Access control & data separation** — every business on JMS Sales App is fully isolated. Your team only ever sees your business's data, never another business's, regardless of what plan or role they hold.

## H. Reports & Analytics

### Analytics (bottom navigation → **Analytics**)

- Key numbers at a glance: total sales, transaction count, average sale, highest/lowest sale.
- **Top Products** — ranked by revenue for the period you're viewing.
- **Gold/Silver/Bronze** badges on the Sales screen highlight your best-selling products over the trailing 30 days (if enabled in Settings).
- Choose **date ranges** to look at today, a custom range, or (with the right permission) any past date.

### Reports (bottom navigation → **Reports**)

- Automatically generated **daily summaries** — gross sales, transaction count, average sale, top product, and top sales person for each business day, plus how it compares to the previous day.
- A **corrections report** covering any voided, corrected, or reversed sales for the day, when applicable.

### Daily Sales PDF

From **History**, tap **Daily Report** next to the date filters to generate a shareable, downloadable PDF summary of the day's sales — ready to save, print, or send.

### Employee / sales-agent performance

Reports and analytics that include "top sales person" reflect who actually recorded each sale, so performance by staff member is visible to anyone with the right permission (typically Supervisors and Administrators).

### Filters

Sales History defaults to **today** and can be filtered by any date range or searched by sale number/product name at any time.

## I. Billing & Subscription

Go to **More → Billing**.

- **Trial status** — every new business starts on a free trial; the Billing page shows how many days remain.
- **Subscription plans** — pick a plan and duration that fits your business; pricing and duration are shown clearly before you confirm.
- **Making a payment** — payments are processed securely through Paystack; you're redirected to complete payment and returned automatically once done.
- **Payment/billing history** — every past payment is listed with its date, amount, and status.
- **What happens when a subscription expires** — the business enters a short grace period, after which access is restricted until a plan is renewed. A Tenant Administrator can always see the current status and renew from this same page.

## K. Inventory & Stock — Full Guide (Optional Module)

Everything about turning it on, setting it up, and using it — for businesses that want to track stock levels alongside their sales.

### Is this for you?

If your business sells countable, stockable goods — a shop, a kiosk, a pharmacy, a hardware store — and you want to know exactly how much of each product you have on hand, when it's running low, and where every unit went, this module is for you. If you sell services or made-to-order items with nothing to track, you can safely ignore it entirely — **the rest of the app works exactly the same either way.**

### Turning it on

1. Go to **More → Settings → Modules**.
2. Switch on **Inventory**.
3. A short **2-day (48-hour) free trial** starts automatically — no payment needed to try it.
4. A new **Stock** tab appears in your bottom navigation right away.

The Inventory module is billed as its own subscription, **completely separate** from your base JMS Sales App plan (section I). Turning either one on or off never affects the other.

### Pricing

Pick whichever duration fits your business — pricing is shown clearly before you confirm.

| Plan | Duration | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly** | 30 days | KES 1,000 |
| **Quarterly** | 90 days | KES 2,500 |
| **6 Months** | 180 days | KES 4,800 |
| **Annual** | 365 days | KES 8,000 |

### Turning tracking on for a product

Once the module is on, open any product from **More → Products** and switch on **Track inventory for this product**. You can then set:

- **Unit of measure** — pieces, kg, litres, boxes, or a custom unit you define.
- **Low-stock threshold** — the balance at which this product starts showing as low stock.
- **SKU / barcode** (optional) — for your own reference.

Only products with tracking switched on appear in Stock — everything else in your catalog is unaffected.

### The Stock dashboard

Bottom navigation → **Stock**, once the module is on. A card list of every tracked product with its current balance, and a clear low-stock badge for anything at or below its threshold. Tap any product for its full balance and movement history.

### Recording stock movements

From a product's detail screen, record any of the following. A reason is required for every adjustment, damage, expiry, and loss entry — so every change to a balance stays explained, not just logged as a number.

| Action | What it does | Reason required? |
|---|---|---|
| **Set opening stock** | Record your starting balance when you first turn on tracking for a product. | No |
| **Stock in** | New stock received — a delivery, a restock. | No |
| **Stock out** | Stock leaving for a reason other than a sale — e.g. moved to another location. | No |
| **Adjust stock up / down** | Correct a balance that's known to be wrong. | Yes |
| **Mark damaged** | Stock that can no longer be sold due to damage. | Yes |
| **Mark expired** | Stock past its usable date. | Yes |
| **Mark lost/missing** | Stock unaccounted for. | Yes |

Every recorded sale of a tracked product automatically reduces its balance too — you never have to double-enter a sale as a separate stock-out.

### Daily reconciliation

Go to **Stock → Reconcile** to compare your system balance against a real physical count for each tracked product, once a day. Enter the actual count; any difference (a variance) is recorded automatically with the product, date, and size of the gap — a simple, honest way to catch shrinkage or counting mistakes early.

### Stock reports

Go to **Stock → Reports** for a 30-day view: a stock-in-vs-out trend chart, a variance report from your reconciliations, and a low-stock list — everything you need to review at a glance, without digging through individual product histories.

### Turning it off

Switch Inventory back off any time from **More → Settings → Modules**. This only hides the Stock tab and its screens — **nothing is cancelled, deleted, or lost.** Turn it back on later and every balance and history picks up exactly where you left off.
